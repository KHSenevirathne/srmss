3   Detailed Weekly Breakdown
18 – 24 May

Week 1 — Research & Requirements

  • Define the problem: manual, spreadsheet-based depot route planning.
  • Study existing fleet-management solutions and industry best practice.
  • Identify target users and gather functional + non-functional requirements.
  • Decide the technology stack (Laravel + Livewire + Flux UI).
  • Output: Requirements Specification document.
25 – 31 May

Week 2 — Planning & System Design

  • Project plan, task breakdown and this timeline.
  • Database schema, ER diagram, use-case and architecture diagrams.
  • Role / permission matrix and UI design system (app.css).
  • Wireframe key screens; initialise repository and dev environment.
  • Output: Design document + initialised codebase.
01 – 11 Jun

Weeks 3–4 — Core Development

  • Authentication & authorisation (Fortify, 2FA, password confirmation).
  • User management, roles, route & stop planning with interactive map.
  • Vehicle / driver records; fuel & maintenance logs with service-due logic.
  • Scheduling with conflict detection, trips board, dashboard & network map.
  • Reporting engine with PDF export and activity / audit logging.
  • Output: Feature-complete SRMSS application.
11 – 14 Jun

Testing & Quality Assurance

  • Unit and feature tests with Pest; lint checks (Pint).
  • Manual test checklist and role-based access-control verification.
14 – 15 Jun

Bug Fixing & Refinement

  • Resolve defects found in testing; refine security settings & 2FA.
  • Restrict profile / security settings access by role; final regression pass.
15 – 16 Jun

Documentation, Deployment & Submission

  • Complete user guide, final report and diagrams.
  • Prepare deployment package (InfinityFree) and deploy.
  • Final review and coursework submission.
